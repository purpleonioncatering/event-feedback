What good feedback looks like

  • Facts, not feelings. A number beats an adjective.
  • Name the item exactly as it reads on the production worksheet.
  • Temperature? Give the actual reading.
  • Personnel problems don’t go here.
Good — “2 of 4 induction burners dead on arrival” Weak — “equipment was a mess”
Good — “Short rib arrived at 118°F, held 40 min in the hotbox” Weak — “beef was cold”
Good — “Sent 180 crab cakes for 220 covers, ran out at 9:15” Weak — “we ran short on apps”
